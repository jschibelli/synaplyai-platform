# SynaplyAI: Team Roles and Responsibilities

This document outlines the detailed roles and responsibilities for each team member within SynaplyAI. Each role is aligned with the platform's strategic goals and includes clear performance expectations.

---
## **Head of Customer Experience (CXO)**

### **Role Overview:**
The **Head of Customer Experience (CXO)** for SynaplyAI will be responsible for overseeing customer success, customer support, and the overall user experience. The CXO will define and implement strategies to improve customer satisfaction, retention, and engagement while ensuring a seamless user journey.

### **Key Responsibilities:**

#### 1. **Customer Experience Strategy and Vision**
- Develop a comprehensive customer experience strategy aligned with business goals.  
- Define customer journey and key interaction points.  
- Develop customer feedback mechanisms to improve user satisfaction.  
- Ensure customer-centric thinking across all teams.  

#### 2. **Customer Support and Success**
- Develop and implement a customer support framework.  
- Oversee onboarding, customer education, and product adoption.  
- Ensure timely resolution of customer issues and support tickets.  
- Monitor customer health scores and satisfaction rates.  

#### 3. **Retention and Engagement**
- Develop strategies to increase customer retention.  
- Implement upselling and cross-selling strategies.  
- Identify customer pain points and resolve them proactively.  
- Build a customer community and engagement platform.  

#### 4. **Cross-Team Collaboration**
- Partner with the CMO to align customer communication and branding.  
- Work with the CPO to align product features with customer expectations.  
- Collaborate with the CTO to improve technical customer support.  
- Provide customer feedback to the AI/ML team to improve AI-driven features.  

---

### **Onboarding Plan:**
1. **First 7 Days:** Meet with the CEO and executive team to align on customer goals.  
2. **First 30 Days:** Develop a customer experience strategy and define key metrics.  
3. **First 60 Days:** Implement customer feedback systems and support improvements.  
4. **First 90 Days:** Present a customer satisfaction and retention report to the executive team.  

---

### **KPIs:**
📌 **Customer Retention:** > 90% customer retention rate.  
📌 **Support Resolution:** > 95% of customer issues resolved within 24 hours.  
📌 **Net Promoter Score (NPS):** > 80% positive customer feedback.  
📌 **Upsell Rate:** > 20% increase in upselling opportunities.  
📌 **Onboarding Success:** > 95% success rate for new customer onboarding.  

---

### **Reporting Structure:**
- Reports to: **CEO**  
- Direct Reports:  
   - **Customer Success Manager** – Oversees customer onboarding and success.  
   - **Support Manager** – Handles customer support operations.  
   - **Community Manager** – Builds customer engagement and feedback channels.
