## 📅 **Version:** March 22, 2025
## 🏆 **Prepared By:** CFO & Product Lead

---

## 🎯 **Strategic Overview**
SynaplyAI is structured around a multi-tiered, token-based subscription model designed to maximize scalability and profitability. The pricing strategy balances accessible entry points with premium high-margin offerings for enterprise clients, while protecting long-term operational costs.

---

## 💼 **Subscription Tier Structure**
| Tier | Target User | Monthly Price | Annual Price (8% Discount) | Key Features |
|-------|-------------|---------------|-----------------------------|--------------|
| **Free Tier** | Individual developers, small teams | $0 | $0 | Basic AI capabilities, limited usage, no support |
| **Pro Tier** | Growing teams and small businesses | $49 | $539 | Access to enhanced AI models, increased token limits, priority processing |
| **Business Tier** | Mid-sized companies with AI-based content needs | $199 | $2150 | Advanced AI handling, real-time collaboration, token sharing |
| **Enterprise Tier** | Large enterprises with high-volume AI requirements | $5,000+ | $60,000+ | Dedicated infrastructure, premium SLA, custom token limits |

## 💳 **Billing Cycles**
✅ Monthly and annual plans available.
✅ **Annual Discount:** 8% discount (1 month free) for annual commitments.
✅ Annual plans require upfront payment.

---

## 🏢 **Enterprise Contract Terms**
✅ Minimum contract: **$5,000/month**
✅ Infrastructure fee: **$2,500/month** (for custom LLM tuning and dedicated GPU clusters)
✅ Contract term: **12-month minimum**
✅ Volume-based overage discount:
   - After 10M tokens/month → $0.015/token
   - After 20M tokens/month → $0.012/token

---

## 🧪 **Free Tier & Trial Strategy**
✅ Soft cap at **2.5M tokens/month per data center**.
✅ Upgrade pathway after 25K tokens → Intro rate of **$29/month for 3 months**.
✅ 14-day trial for Pro and Business tiers → No credit card required.

## 💡 **Strategic Adjustments Made**
✔️ Increased Pro overage to **$0.03/token** to cover Claude 3.7 costs.  
✔️ Increased Business overage to **$0.02/token** for margin protection.  
✔️ Reduced annual discount from **16% → 8%** to strengthen cash flow.  
✔️ Locked in Enterprise contract at **$5K minimum** with infrastructure fee.  
✔️ Built soft cap + waitlist system for Free-tier cost control.
