After careful consideration of our project status and readiness assessments, I'm writing to share our updated launch timeline and strategy for SynaplyAI.

## Revised Launch Timeline

We've made the strategic decision to implement a phased launch approach:

1. **April 8, 2025:** Landing Page Launch
2. **April 22, 2025:** Limited Beta Launch (50-100 selected customers)
3. **May 6, 2025:** Full Platform Launch

This phased approach prioritizes quality, stability, and customer experience while allowing us to build market anticipation.

I'm pleased to announce we have a **GO** decision for our landing page launch on April 8th. The landing page will establish our market presence, begin collecting qualified leads, and build our beta waitlist while we complete final platform preparations.
