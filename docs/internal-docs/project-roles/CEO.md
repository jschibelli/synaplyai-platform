## 🧠 SynaplyAI – CEO Role Description

### **Title:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
### **Reports To:** Board of Directors  
### **Version:** March 30, 2025

---

### 🏢 **Role Overview**

The CEO of SynaplyAI is the visionary leader responsible for the overall strategy, growth, and execution of the company’s mission: to become the enterprise standard for AI-driven content creation. As SynaplyAI transitions from product development to market launch and growth, the CEO will lead cross-functional teams across engineering, AI/ML, product, legal, compliance, marketing, and sales to deliver operational excellence and strategic market positioning.

---

### 🌟 **Key Responsibilities**

#### 🚀 Strategic Leadership
- Set and refine SynaplyAI’s long-term vision, goals, and roadmap.
- Lead company-wide OKRs and ensure alignment across departments.
- Prioritize innovation, security, and scalability as core principles in product and business strategy.

#### 🛠️ Product Execution & Delivery
- Oversee the phased execution of SynaplyAI’s multi-tenant platform as detailed in the [project plan](project-plan.md).
- Drive coordination between AI/ML, backend, frontend, and UX/UI leaders to ensure milestone delivery.
- Partner with the CTO and Project Manager to maintain architectural integrity and feature velocity.

#### 💼 Business Operations & Growth
- Lead business development and enterprise partnerships.
- Oversee the monetization strategy via the finalized [pricing model](Pricing-strategy.md).
- Approve enterprise deals, manage investor relations, and ensure profitability and ARR growth targets.

#### 📢 Brand & Market Strategy
- Represent SynaplyAI publicly, driving the thought leadership agenda across developer, enterprise, and AI communities.
- Oversee marketing and public relations with the CMO to support SynaplyAI’s launch phases.
- Guide product positioning, customer acquisition, and competitive differentiation strategies.

#### 🛡️ Governance & Compliance
- Ensure adherence to legal, compliance, and data protection frameworks (e.g., SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA).
- Collaborate with Compliance & Legal Lead on enterprise agreements and audit-readiness.

#### 🢑 Team Leadership
- Build and retain an exceptional leadership team across engineering, product, marketing, legal, and operations.
- Drive performance culture and cross-team collaboration with transparency and accountability.
